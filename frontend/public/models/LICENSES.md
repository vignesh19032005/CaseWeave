# Third-party 3D models

All models are from the Khronos Group glTF Sample Assets (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets), used unmodified, each under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

| File | Used for | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| ChairDamaskPurplegold.glb | a chair, only when the evidence describes one (not assumed) | (c) 2021 Wayfair |
| CesiumMilkTruck.glb | a truck / van / lorry, only when the evidence describes one | (c) 2017 Cesium |
| CesiumMan.glb | the walking figure for people in the scene | (c) 2017 Cesium |

Everything else in the scene is drawn from simple shapes generated in code. Objects the evidence does not describe (assumed) are always drawn as plain shapes with a red outline, never with a model.
The people are a single generic figure. Walking follows the real distance travelled; crouch, sit, lie and struggle are stylised poses of that one figure, not recorded motion.

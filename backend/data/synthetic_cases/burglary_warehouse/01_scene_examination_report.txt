Case reference: SYN-BURG-01 (synthetic demonstration case)
Examiner: Sub-Inspector A. Marlowe, Scene Examination Unit
Examination date: 12 March 2026, 07:40 to 09:55
Premises: Kestrel Trade Supplies, Unit 4, Dockside Industrial Park (fictional)

1. THE PREMISES
The unit is a single-storey building with a loading yard on its east side. The ground floor has three rooms:
- Trade counter and showroom, about 8 m wide by 6 m deep, at the front (south) of the building. The customer door opens onto the car park.
- Stockroom, about 10 m wide by 8 m deep, behind the showroom (north). It is joined to the showroom by one internal door, 1 m wide, in the middle of the dividing wall. A steel racking aisle runs down the stockroom, 1 m wide and 2.2 m high shelving, on the west side.
- Small office, about 4 m by 4 m, in the north-east corner of the building, opening off the stockroom through a door on the stockroom's east wall. It holds a desk, a steel filing cabinet and a wall safe (grey, 0.5 m by 0.5 m by 0.5 m) bolted to the north wall.
A side window, 1.2 m wide and 1 m high with its sill 1.1 m above the ground, is set in the west wall of the stockroom, facing a narrow service lane. A roller loading door, 3 m wide, is in the east wall of the stockroom facing the loading yard; it was closed and locked.

2. CONDITION ON ARRIVAL
- The side window in the stockroom west wall had its frame prised open. The pane was intact; the lock keep was torn from the frame. Tool marks about 12 mm wide are on the frame. A pry bar was left on the ground under the window.
- Muddy partial footwear marks (size 9 to 10 sole pattern) lead from the window to the stockroom door and then to the office door.
- The office door was open. The filing cabinet drawers were pulled out. The wall safe was found open and empty. Its keypad shows no damage.
- Three pallets of boxed power tools on the racking aisle had been moved. Two stacked boxes were on the floor at the aisle's south end, as if dropped.
- The showroom and the front customer door were undisturbed. The loading door was locked and undamaged.

3. ITEMS TAKEN (AS REPORTED BY THE OWNER)
Cash float of about 6,400 in the safe, and approximately 20 boxed cordless drills from the racking.

4. EXAMINER'S NOTE
The entry point is the stockroom west window. Two people seem to have been involved: two sets of footwear marks of different size were found, and the quantity of tools taken suggests two people carrying.

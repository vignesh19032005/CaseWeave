INCIDENT REPORT
Case: CW-001
Reporting Officer: (fictional) SI D. Vasanth
Date filed: 2026-10-20

Summary: On the night of 19 October 2026, a complaint was lodged by the warehouse
supervisor of Srinivasan Logistics that one sealed carton (Item ref: SL-INV-224,
declared contents: electronic components) was found missing from the storage
rack nearest the side entrance during the morning stock check on 20 October 2026.

Evidence reviewed: CCTV footage from Camera CAM-02 (side entrance), side-door
access control log (device ACC-DOOR-02), two witness statements (R. Kumaran,
P. Elango), and a visual inspection of the storage rack and door area.

Observations: The access log shows the side door opening at 21:14 and again at
23:40. The CCTV camera overlay places a person entering at approximately 21:17.
Witness R. Kumaran reports seeing a person enter the side entrance "around
9:10 PM, maybe a few minutes before or after." Witness P. Elango reports the
door being open and checked the time on his phone at 9:21 PM.

Limitation: no camera was present covering the interior storage rack itself, so
there is no direct visual record of the carton being removed. The exact point
at which the carton went missing cannot be established from video alone.

Missing evidence: the warehouse's interior motion-sensor log, which supervisor
states exists but has not yet been retrieved from building management at time
of this report.

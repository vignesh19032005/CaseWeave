1. POINT OF ENTRY & PERIMETER OBSERVATIONS
The target premises occupies the ground floor of a commercial building. Initial inspection revealed the front glass entrance was intact and secured with internal electric shutter locks. Physical point of entry was established at the rear service alleyway shutter (designated Gate-B). The center padlock had been compromised by physical torsion using an iron lever/crowbar. Striation marks measuring 18mm width were lifted from the locking clasp. Fresh metal filings and paint flakes were recovered from the threshold.

2. INVENTORY & STOCKROOM ANALYSIS
In the secured internal dispensary (Schedule H1 / Narcotics storage area), a dual-door steel locker (Godrej Model SEC-40) showed severe mechanical deformation. The mechanical combination dial was sheared off. The following schedule drugs were noted missing per stock register comparison:
- 120 ampoules of Fentanyl Citrate injection (50mcg/ml)
- 400 tablets of Alprazolam (0.5mg strip packs)
- 85 vials of Ketamine Hydrochloride (50mg/ml)
Counter cash till was ransacked; approximately INR 42,500 collected during the previous evening shift was confirmed missing.

3. PHYSICAL EXHIBITS RECOVERED
- Exhibit Q-1: One (1) 18-inch hex-shank iron crowbar bearing yellow tip paint, recovered from stockroom aisle 3.
- Exhibit Q-2: One (1) left-hand blue powder-free nitrile examination glove with friction tear on index finger.
- Exhibit Q-3: Latent partial fingerprint lifted from glass display shelf #2 (forwarded to State Fingerprint Bureau).

4. PRELIMINARY FORENSIC OPINION
Entry was executed by individuals possessing knowledge of the pharmacy floor plan and storage of restricted pharmaceutical substances. Forced entry at the rear shutter occurred between 02:15 hrs and 03:00 hrs on 28-10-2026.
